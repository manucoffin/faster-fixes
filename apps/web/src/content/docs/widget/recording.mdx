## What it is

A Recording works like a session replay, scoped to a single Feedback: it covers up to about a minute on the page before the Reviewer selected an element. Only Reviewers are recorded, never the other visitors of your site.

The widget keeps a buffer of the recent past while the page is open and attaches it when the Feedback is submitted, with no extra step for the Reviewer. A Recording is a reconstruction of the page, not a video of the screen, and holds no audio.

## What is captured

- The page's content as it changed: its structure, text, styles, and the URLs of its images.
- The Reviewer's clicks, scrolling and pointer movements.

## What is never captured

- The value of every `input`, `textarea` and `select`, whatever its type, and the text of every `contenteditable` element. The Recording shows that the Reviewer typed, and where, but not what. There is no way to unmask these fields.
- The widget's own interface.
- Anything before the last full page load, or before a return to the page through the browser's back or forward buttons.
- Canvas content, video frames, and the content of third-party iframes.

## Masking and blocking content

Two attributes let your site keep more out of the Recording. Each applies to the element and to all its descendants. A descendant cannot be exempted.

`data-ff-mask` replaces the text of the element with `*`. The layout is kept.

```html
<section data-ff-mask>
  <h2>Account</h2>
  <p>Jane Doe, jane@example.com</p>
</section>
```

Set it on `<body>` to mask all text on the page:

```html
<body data-ff-mask></body>
```

`data-ff-block` replaces the element with an empty placeholder of the same size. The element is also left out of the Screenshot.

```html
<div data-ff-block>
  <img src="/statements/2026-10.png" alt="Bank statement" />
</div>
```

## The Screenshot

Masking does not apply to the Screenshot. The Screenshot still shows input values and the text of `data-ff-mask` elements. Only `data-ff-block` reaches it: a blocked element is removed from the Screenshot.

## Turning it off

Set `captureRecording` to `false`. With the script embed, add `data-capture-recording="false"` to the script tag. The recorder is then never loaded.

  {children}

Recording is independent of `captureDiagnostics`: turning off the Diagnostic Trail does not turn off Recording, and the reverse. There is no dashboard setting, and the Reviewer has no way to turn it off.

## When a Feedback has no Recording

A Recording is best effort. A Feedback has none when:

- `captureRecording` is `false`,
- the recorder file was blocked, for example by a [Content Security Policy](/docs/widget/content-security-policy), or had not finished loading,
- the browser has no native compression (`CompressionStream`),
- the Recording was over the size cap of 4 MB once compressed,
- the upload failed, or the Reviewer left the page right after submitting, before the upload finished,
- the Feedback was sent with the [headless client](/docs/widget/headless-client), which does not record,
- the Feedback is on a Video.

None of these shows an error: the Feedback is submitted without a Recording.

## Playback limits

Web fonts are not stored with the Recording, and images are fetched from your site at playback. An image or font that was removed, moved or served behind a login can be missing at playback. To fetch them, the browser of the Member who plays a Recording contacts your site, with no referrer. The Screenshot is exact.

## Storage and removal

A Recording is stored with the Feedback's other files and kept as long as the Feedback. Deleting the Feedback deletes it.

Any Member can remove a Recording from the inbox and keep the Feedback. Removal is permanent. It does not reach Issues already mirrored to a Tracker: the timeline written into them stays.

## A sentence for your privacy policy

The example below can be adapted for your site's privacy policy. It is an example, not legal advice: check it against your own obligations.

```text
When you leave feedback with our review tool, it records the page content
and your clicks, scrolling and pointer movements during up to about a
minute before your feedback. Values typed into form fields are never
recorded. This recording is processed by Faster Fixes on our behalf and is
kept with your feedback.
```
